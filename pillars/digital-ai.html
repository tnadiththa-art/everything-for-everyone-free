Digital & Artificial Intelligence

Demystifying modern AI, running open-weight local models on your own hardware for zero dollars, protecting your digital privacy, and mastering essential technology.

Why Digital & AI Literacy?

Core Focus

Artificial intelligence and digital infrastructure shouldn't be locked behind expensive monthly subscriptions or corporate walled gardens. We believe everyone has the right to understand how these systems work, how to run them privately on their own machines, and how to harness their power without surrendering personal data.

💡 Zero Cost Philosophy

Every guide in this pillar uses 100% free, open-weight, or open-source software. You will never need a paid API key or subscription to follow our tutorials.

Recommended Free & Open Tools

Verified FOSS

Tools we recommend and will feature in forthcoming walkthroughs:

  • Ollama: Effortlessly run open-source large language models locally.
  • LM Studio: Intuitive cross-platform desktop GUI for discovering and testing local GGUF models.
  • Bitwarden: Open-source, audited password management with a generous free plan.
  • Brave / Firefox + uBlock Origin: Fast, ad-free, and tracker-free web browsing.
