About Our Mission & Manifesto

Why we exist, why practical knowledge should remain free forever, and how we build without paywalls or friction.

“Practical knowledge for living, working, and thriving in the modern world is not a luxury product. It is a fundamental public good.”

The Problem With the Modern Web

In recent years, the open web has steadily been carved up into walled gardens. Basic life advice, technical tutorials, and educational guides are routinely trapped behind paywalls, subscription models, intrusive pop-ups, and forced account registrations.

Search results are often bloated with search-engine optimized filler, ad tracking, and corporate sponsored content designed to sell products rather than solve problems. Finding clean, concise, reliable step-by-step guidance has become needlessly difficult.

Core Principles

Our Guiding Tenets

1. Uncompromisingly Free

Zero paywalls, zero premium tiers, and zero affiliate traps. If a guide exists here, it is 100% accessible to every person on Earth with an internet connection.

2. Zero Sign-Ups or Gates

You do not need to give us your email address, create an account, or consent to tracking cookies to learn. The content is immediately available upon loading the page.

3. Practical Over Theoretical

We focus on actionable skills: how to run local AI models without spending money, how to construct a resilient personal budget, how to write an effective resume, and how to stay healthy using everyday routines.

4. Lightweight & Durable

Built with semantic HTML5 and vanilla CSS. No bloated JavaScript frameworks, no heavy tracking bundles, and no unnecessary complexity. Fast on any device, anywhere.

The Five Pillars Framework

Everything for Everyone Free is organized into five foundational pillars designed to cover critical modern capabilities:

  • Digital & AI: Practical tech literacy, privacy, security, and leveraging modern AI models locally without subscription lock-in.
  • Skills & Career: Resume building, interview strategies, self-taught portfolios, and modern career navigation.
  • Life & Finance: Realistic personal finance, debt paydown methods, emergency funds, and frugal consumer wisdom.
  • Health & Habits: Calisthenics, sleep hygiene, mental resilience, and budget-friendly home cooking.
  • Free Tools & Software: A curated index of battle-tested Free and Open Source Software (FOSS) and ethical alternatives.

Open Source & Community-Owned

This project is open-source and maintained in the open. As we launch upcoming guides, community contributions and suggestions will help keep our content accurate, unbiased, and accessible.
